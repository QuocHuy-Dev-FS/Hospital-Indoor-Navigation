# Quân y 7A — Hospital wayfinding

## Direction (2026-10-26)
The user's two reference images govern the redesign: teal/white medical website shell and a detailed open-roof dollhouse model. Adopt the website's photographic medical introduction, legible navigation and curved white lower edge. The application itself stays an Operate surface with the map dominant.

## Visual system
Self-hosted Be Vietnam Pro. Main action #087f70 with white text; ground #f5faf8; text #203d38. Authentic rectangular hospital logo retains its red/green colors on white. Teal hero/photo overlay is explicitly derived from the supplied reference, not a generic decoration.

## 3D model
Warm white walls, pale tiled floors, wood desks/doors, muted teal medical upholstery, steel fixtures, plants, stairs and elevators. Cut front/interior walls low to show furnishings; retain taller rear perimeter. Sun, environment lighting, static soft shadows and local contact shading provide depth. Instance repeated geometry/materials per floor. No images substitute for the interactive model.

## Behavior
One three-floor selector. Picking a room highlights its floor without auto-flying the camera; Xem gần and Toàn cảnh are explicit actions. Drag/pinch does not select a room. Interior visibility is separate from architecture. Existing route and wheelchair controls stay working; mobile prioritizes the model and route form.

## Provenance and content
Keep truthful trial labels, source room names and source geometry. Show an illustrative-interior note. No unverified clinical service labels or fabricated doctor data. No third-party image/font requests at runtime.
