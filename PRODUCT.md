# Hospital Indoor Navigation — Quân y 7A

## Platform
Web prototype, Vite and Three.js; local development at localhost:5173.

## Users and task
Patients and visitors find a room, choose their starting room and follow a route. Wheelchair mode and readable Vietnamese remain available. Real audience/device requirements still need field validation.

## Confirmed scope
Three display floors: Tầng trệt (F1), Tầng 1 (F2), Tầng 2 (F3). Keep source IDs, room bounds, coordinates and graph references. Position is manual/simulated; real BLE integration is unfinished. No camera or account required.

## Current user brief
User requested direct, uninterrupted implementation: realistic cutaway hospital interiors inspired by supplied dollhouse photograph; medical website in teal/white inspired by supplied web screenshot; authentic Quân y 7A logo. Preserve stable controls and existing three-floor navigation.

## Truth and limits
Floor plans and interiors are provisional. Furnishings illustrate a hospital style and are not surveyed medical equipment or real room assignments. No invented clinical service claims, doctors, testimonials or contact details.
